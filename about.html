Our story

Made of place,
made personal.

Pradhan began with a family home, a love of the hills and a belief that the best stays feel a little like coming back.

Welcome in

A home in the
green quiet.

We opened our doors to share the part of Darjeeling we love most: the slower side, where mornings stretch, meals are shared and the weather gives you a reason to stay in.

Our promise is simple. Keep the rooms comfortable, the food honest and the welcome warm. Help you find the places that do not always make the guidebook. Leave the hills better than we found them.

Our commitment to guests

From high-speed WiFi and thoughtful room amenities to a host who remembers how you take your tea, we pay attention to the small things. We work with local suppliers, recommend local makers and encourage low-impact ways to explore.

Come as a guest

Stay for the view, the food, the quiet, or for no particular reason at all. There is always room at our table.

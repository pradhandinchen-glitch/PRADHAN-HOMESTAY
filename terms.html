Reservations and deposit

Paid online booking requests are subject to availability and are not confirmed until Pradhan Homestay confirms them. A ₹1,000 deposit is collected online for each request; the remaining stay balance is payable at the homestay.

If a request cannot be confirmed

If the host cannot confirm the requested dates, the ₹1,000 deposit will be held as credit for another date. Contact the host to arrange a replacement booking; credit redemption is currently coordinated manually.

Arrival and departure

Standard check-in is from 2 pm and check-out is by 11 am. Early arrival and late departure can be requested in advance and are subject to availability.

Cancellation

Cancellation terms for a confirmed stay will be shared by the host at confirmation and may vary by season, room type and package.

Respect for the home

Guests are asked to respect quiet hours, shared spaces and the surrounding neighbourhood. Any damage may be charged at replacement cost.

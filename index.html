Pradhan Homestay sits in the green fold of Badamtam, Naya Busty, Lebong — close enough to Darjeeling's energy, far enough to hear the first birds.

Stay for a weekend of mountain air, a family celebration, or a few quiet days with a book. We keep things personal: home-cooked food, thoughtful rooms, and a view that never needs a filter.
